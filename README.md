# Build Your Own Agents with Microsoft 365 Copilot

Welcome! In these hands-on labs, you will build AI agents for Tailwind Traders, a simulated health and beauty retailer.

- **Session 1 – Agent Builder:** Build agents that answer questions and analyse data, and extend their capabilities with skills, all without writing code.
- **Session 2 – Copilot Studio:** Take agents further by enabling them to collect information, apply business rules, and take action.


> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Tailwind Traders** is a fictitious company name used in Microsoft demos and training. In these labs it is a **simulated health and beauty retailer**. All policies, people, figures and data are **fictional** and created for training only. They do not represent any real company policy, store or result.

---

## About the Tailwind Traders

**Tailwind Traders** is a health and beauty retailer operating both physical stores and an online shop. It offers a wide range of products, including skincare, cosmetics, personal care, health and wellness products, and its own-brand collections.

Customers can shop in-store or online, and many are members of the **Tailwind Rewards** loyalty programme.

The company is now preparing for its biggest promotional event of the year, **Mega Sale Week**, bringing exciting deals and special offers to customers across all shopping channels.

Behind every store visit, many teams work together:

| Team | What they do |
|---|---|
| **Store Operations** | Run the stores day to day: displays, checkout, stock and customer service on the floor |
| **Trading & Merchandising** | Choose products, plan stock and set up promotions with suppliers |
| **Marketing** | Plan campaigns, social media and member communications |
| **Customer Service** | Answer customer questions and complaints across channels |
| **HR** | Look after people: staffing, leave, benefits and training |
| **Finance** | Track sales, margins and promotion results |

### The event: Mega Sale Week

Every year, from 1 to 7 December, Tailwind Traders runs **Mega Sale Week**, a 7-day storewide sale (fictional). It includes storewide discounts, **double Tailwind Rewards points** and limited-edition bundles. You can do these labs at any time of year: the sale dates repeat every year, and no lab depends on a specific year.

For the business, this is the busiest week of the year. **Every team is involved at the same time** and everyone has questions:

- Store Operations: *"When does the display need to be ready?"* 
- Trading & Merchandising: *"Which items are excluded from the discount?"* 
- Marketing, Customer Service: *"Can members use an e-coupon on top of the sale price?"* 
- Finance: *"How did day one perform?"*
- Everyone: *"Can I take annual leave during the sale?"* 


> That makes it the perfect place to see how AI agents can help.

## How the labs are organised

Each **use case** is one agent. You build it in **Session 1**, and selected use cases continue in **Session 2**.

| # | Use case | What the agent does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Ask HR](docs/use-case-1-ask-hr/index.md) | Answers leave and benefits questions from the HR guide, and helps employees prepare leave requests that follow the rules |
| 2 | [Mega Sale Assistant](docs/use-case-2-mega-sale-assistant/index.md) | Answers Mega Sale questions from the official sale documents, and gives store teams step-by-step procedures for store problems |
| 3 | [Review Insights Helper](docs/use-case-3-review-insights-helper/index.md) | Finds the top issues and praise in customer reviews, flags health and safety concerns, and helps the team act on them |
| 4 | [Sale Performance Analyst](docs/use-case-4-sale-performance-analyst/index.md) | Analyses Mega Sale sales data using Tailwind Traders' business rules, and creates the daily flash report for Management |

> [!TIP]
> **One agent, many teams.** Each use case lists the questions that **different teams** might ask the same agent. Pick the questions closest to your own role when you test.
