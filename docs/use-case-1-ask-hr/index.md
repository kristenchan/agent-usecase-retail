# Use Case 1 – Ask HR

[← Back to home](../../README.md)

## Scenario

HR spends about **20% of each day** answering the same leave questions. The answers are in a long HR guide, and some rules depend on whether you work in a store or at head office. Employees also need help writing leave requests before they submit them in **MyHR**, the company's HR system.

It gets even busier before **Mega Sale Week**, when HR hears the same questions from every part of the business:

- *Can I take leave during the sale?*
- *Can I take time off instead of overtime pay?*
- *Can I use my staff discount on sale items?*

## Why this agent?

Build **Ask HR**, an agent that **answers leave and benefits questions from the HR guide, and helps employees prepare leave requests that follow the rules.**

- **Who it's for:** Every employee: store staff, store managers and head office teams
- **What it uses:** The Employee Leave & Benefits Guide
- **What it does:**
  - Answers leave and benefits questions, and names the section of the guide
  - Asks whether you work in a store or at head office when the rules differ
  - Drafts a leave request and checks it against the rules
- **What changes:** Employees get answers in seconds, any time. HR answers fewer repeated questions, and leave requests follow the rules the first time.
