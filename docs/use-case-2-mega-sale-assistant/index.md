# Use Case 2 – Mega Sale Assistant

[← Back to home](../../README.md)

## Scenario

The Store Operations team at head office answers about **50 questions a day** from stores, by phone and Teams. The answers are spread across a playbook, a store guide and an FAQ, so people call or message head office and wait for a reply.

It gets even busier during **Mega Sale Week**, when the number of questions triples:

- *When must the display be ready?*
- *Which items are excluded?*
- *Can a coupon be used on top of the sale price?*

## Why this agent?

Build **Mega Sale Assistant**, an agent that **answers Mega Sale questions from the official sale documents, and gives store teams step-by-step procedures for store problems.**

- **Who it's for:** Everyone working on Mega Sale Week: store teams, store managers and head office teams such as Store Operations, Trading & Merchandising, Marketing, Finance and Customer Service
- **What it uses:** The Mega Sale Playbook, the Store Execution Guide and the Mega Sale FAQ
- **What it does:**
  - Answers questions about the sale, and names the source document
  - Gives store teams a step-by-step procedure for store problems: what to do now, who approves and when to escalate
  - Says when the documents don't cover a question, and suggests who to ask
- **What changes:** Every team has one place to ask. Head office gets fewer repeated calls and messages, and every store handles the same problem in the same way.
