# Use Case 3 – Review Insights Helper

[← Back to home](../../README.md)

## Scenario

The Customer Experience team spends about **half a day every week** reading customer reviews and turning them into a report for leadership. With so many reviews, important signals are easy to miss:

- a product with repeated complaints
- stores with long queues
- a review about a skin reaction that needs action

It gets even busier during **Mega Sale Week**, when about 150 reviews arrive in just 7 days.

## Why this agent?

Build **Review Insights Helper**, an agent that **finds the top issues and praise in customer reviews, flags health and safety concerns, and helps the team act on them.**

- **Who it's for:** Marketing, Trading & Merchandising, Store Operations, Customer Service, Quality and Management
- **What it uses:** The Mega Sale customer reviews (an Excel file of about 150 reviews)
- **What it does:**
  - Finds the top complaints and praise, with charts
  - Ranks the issues and names the team that owns each one
  - Always flags health and safety concerns, even in reviews with a high rating
  - Creates the review report for leadership in a fixed layout
- **What changes:** Reviews are analysed in minutes instead of hours. Health and safety concerns aren't missed, each issue reaches the right team, and leadership gets the same report layout every time.
